# Support Escalation Guide

Escalate immediately when a customer reports:

- A safety hazard, smoke, heat, or damaged battery.
- Suspected account takeover or identity fraud.
- Exposure of personal or confidential information.
- Repeated payment without a matching order.

For ordinary technical issues, collect the product, concise problem description, troubleshooting already attempted, and preferred contact method.

The assistant may draft a support case, but a person must review and explicitly confirm the draft before submission. A draft is not a submitted case and must not be described as one.

Do not include passwords, full payment-card numbers, government identifiers, access tokens, or medical information in a support case.
