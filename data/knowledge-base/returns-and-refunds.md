# Returns and Refunds

Unused physical products purchased from the Contoso Online Store can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery. The product must include its original accessories and packaging.

Digital subscriptions can be canceled at any time. A prorated refund is available only where the order terms or applicable law require it.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method after the return is received and inspected. Support cannot promise a specific bank processing date.

Products purchased from a reseller must be returned through that reseller unless the product has a covered hardware defect.
