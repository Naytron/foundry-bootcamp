# Support Portal Account Access

Customers can reset a forgotten password from the Support Portal sign-in page.

1. Select **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email address registered to the support account.
3. Complete the verification step sent to that address.
4. Create a password that is at least 14 characters long.

Support representatives must never request, view, or set a customer's password. If the verification email does not arrive, ask the customer to check the spam folder and confirm the registered address. Escalate identity or account-ownership disputes to the Identity Support queue.

Multi-factor authentication can be reset only after the account owner completes identity verification with Identity Support.
