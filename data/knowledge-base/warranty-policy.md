# Hardware Warranty Policy

Contoso Trail Devices include a two-year limited hardware warranty beginning on the original purchase date. The warranty covers manufacturing defects under normal use.

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, loss, cosmetic wear, unauthorized repair, or damage caused by use outside the environmental limits in the product guide.

A warranty claim requires the device serial number and proof of purchase. A support case can be drafted before those details are available, but eligibility cannot be confirmed until both are provided.

Customers in a region with stronger statutory protections keep those rights. The limited warranty does not replace rights provided by applicable consumer law.
